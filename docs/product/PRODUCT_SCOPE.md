# PRODUCT SCOPE

**Project:** Sofia Web Extractor (SWE)

**Status:** Active

**Version:** 1.0

---

# Product Definition

The Sofia Web Extractor (SWE) is the first module of the SOFIA ecosystem.

Its purpose is to capture digital interactions, preserve the generated knowledge and provide efficient retrieval of that information.

---

# Included in Version 1.x

- Authentication
- Knowledge extraction
- Task management
- File management
- Search
- Automatic configuration (SWE-Start)

---

# Explicitly Out of Scope

The following capabilities belong to future SOFIA modules and are not part of the SWE:

- Knowledge curation
- Semantic vectorization
- Autonomous reasoning
- Pattern discovery
- Knowledge management
- AI orchestration

---

# Relationship with SOFIA

The SWE is a complete standalone product.

Within the SOFIA ecosystem, it acts as the knowledge ingestion layer.
