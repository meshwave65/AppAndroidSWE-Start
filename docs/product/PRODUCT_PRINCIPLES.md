# PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

**Project:** Sofia Web Extractor (SWE)

**Status:** Active

**Version:** 1.0

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-02

---

# Purpose

This document defines the principles that guide every technical and product decision within the Sofia Web Extractor (SWE).

These principles take precedence over implementation details and are intended to preserve the product's identity throughout its evolution.

---

# Core Mission

The SWE exists to:

- Capture knowledge.
- Preserve knowledge.
- Recover knowledge.

Every feature must reinforce at least one of these three objectives.

---

# Product Principles

## 1. Solve real problems.

Technology is a means, never the objective.

---

## 2. Simplicity over complexity.

Additional complexity must always produce proportional value.

---

## 3. User knowledge belongs to the user.

The SWE stores and organizes knowledge but never claims ownership of it.

---

## 4. Transparency builds trust.

Whenever information leaves the user's environment, the user must know exactly what is being transmitted.

---

## 5. Every new feature must justify its existence.

Before implementing any feature, ask:

> Does the absence of this feature prevent the SWE from fulfilling its core mission?

If the answer is no, it probably belongs to a future version.

---

## 6. The product evolves incrementally.

Each release must deliver meaningful value instead of accumulating superficial changes.

---

## 7. The main branch must always remain stable.

Experimental work belongs to development branches.
